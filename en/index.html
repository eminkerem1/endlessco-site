ENDLESS.CO is an Ankara-based professional services brand that brings together academics, trainers, researchers and project specialists. We help organisations develop project ideas, plan funding applications, deliver training and run research and evaluation work.

WHAT WE OFFER

Nine service packages

01

First Step

Project and funding opportunity assessment: two online meetings, a review of up to three funding programmes and a 2–3 page written roadmap. Fixed fee: TRY 1,900 (VAT included).

02

Project Design Workshop

A hands-on workshop in which your team turns its own project idea into a structured project design.

03

Project Implementation and Training

Training, workshops, expert coordination and evaluation for funded projects, within the rules of each programme.

04

Applied Competence Programmes

Tailored, practice-based training: AI and digital productivity, office and digital tools, project cycle management (PCM), communication, entrepreneurship.

05

Experiential Learning and Adventure Education

Team, leadership and communication development through structured group tasks, simulations and low-risk outdoor activities, run by qualified facilitators.

06

Organisational Needs and Stakeholder Research

Surveys, interviews, focus groups and workshops that turn stakeholder views into actionable recommendations.

07

Impact and Evaluation

Monitoring and evaluation frameworks, pre/post assessments and outcome reports, with clear methodological limits on impact claims.

08

Academic Mentoring

Research design and methodology guidance for graduate students and researchers. We never write theses or papers on anyone’s behalf.

09

Qualitative and Quantitative Data Analysis

Statistical and thematic analysis of research data, with results explained to the researcher.

Apart from First Step, all services are priced through a written, tailored proposal. Detailed descriptions are available on our Turkish service pages.
