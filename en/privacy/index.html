How we process personal data when you visit our website or contact us. Last updated: 11 November 2026.

About this notice

This is an English summary of our Turkish privacy notice (KVKK Aydınlatma Metni), prepared under Article 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK). If the two versions differ, the Turkish version prevails.

Data controller

Aleyna ERKADAM, owner of the sole proprietorship operating under the ENDLESS.CO brand. Address: Kızılırmak Mahallesi, 1450. Sokak No: 16, Çankaya/Ankara, Türkiye · Email: endlesscotr@gmail.com.

What we process and how

Name, email address, organisation, the package or service you are interested in and the content of your message, collected directly from you through the contact form or by email; and technical records such as IP address and time of request, created automatically by our hosting and form service providers.

Purposes and legal bases

  • Responding to your enquiry and preparing proposals (KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)).
  • Answering general questions and keeping our services secure (KVKK Art. 5(2)(f), legitimate interest).
  • Meeting legal obligations and protecting our rights (KVKK Art. 5(2)(ç) and (e)).

We do not use your data for marketing.

Recipients and transfers abroad

We share only what is necessary with the experts working on your request, and use FormSubmit (form delivery), Google (email) and GitHub (website hosting) as service providers. Their servers are located outside Türkiye, so your data is transferred abroad solely to provide these services, subject to Article 9 of the KVKK.

Retention

Enquiries that do not lead to a contract are kept for up to two years from our last correspondence. If a contract is concluded, records are kept for the periods required by Turkish commercial and tax law.

Cookies

Our website does not use cookies or tracking tools.

Your rights

Under Article 11 of the KVKK you may ask whether your data is processed, request information, correction or deletion, learn who it has been shared with, object to certain automated decisions and claim compensation for unlawful processing. To exercise your rights, write to endlesscotr@gmail.com from the email address you used to contact us. You also have the right to complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board.
